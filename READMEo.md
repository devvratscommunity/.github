# Devvrats

### Learn together. Build together.

Devvrats is a community for developers and creators who believe that the best way to grow is to learn, build, and share knowledge together.

We bring curious minds together to explore technology, collaborate on projects, and contribute to a culture where everyone has room to grow.

**[Explore Devvrats](https://www.devvrats.in/)** · **[Join the Community](https://tally.so/r/Pd0O0d)** · **[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/devvratscommunity/)**

---

## What we're about

- **Learning:** Grow your skills through shared knowledge, exploration, and practice.
- **Building:** Turn ideas into meaningful projects through collaboration.
- **Open source:** Learn by contributing, experimenting, and creating together.
- **Community:** Connect with developers and creators who share your curiosity.

## Our philosophy

You don't need to know everything to get started. You just need the willingness to learn, the courage to build, and the openness to grow with others.

At Devvrats, progress matters more than perfection.
